# V3 – final-style website pilot handoff

30 October 2026. Source owner: build_stk_clickable_prototype. Publication remains with parent. `dist/` is the authoritative static build; no build dependencies. Preview: http://127.0.0.1:4173/.

## What changed
- Visitor-first public information architecture; no public staff/admin mode switch. Contextual account entry, independent internal demo routes.
- Original navy/pale-blue identity, original logo and image assets, locally served IBM Plex Sans / Plus Jakarta Sans. Deliberate H1–H4 scale, readable leads and consistent spacing.
- Main content and contextual assistant share a measured grid. On narrow screens the assistant follows the introductory content in normal flow, not an overlay. Shared visitor brief follows application and applicant journey; staff contexts are separated.
- Real source housing: six property/room-type source records, 17 published room rows, 28 correctly associated gallery photos, search/filter/detail/room/enquiry flow. These are source records, not a claim of 17 unique currently available rooms. External applicant evidence gate and honest no-reservation result.
- Four-provider comparison directory, search/filter/compare three explicitly fictional university programmes, source course details, German learning timeline, recognition and mentoring, five examination families and official provider paths.
- Three-step advisory appointment simulation with real published contact/booking destinations. Application retains three sample documents and brief through its final summary and applicant portal.
- All 1,770 captured German knowledge entries are searchable by 15 topics; individual source pages include provenance and legal/visa caution. This is source migration, not fresh factual validation.
- 62 readable source-language editorial pages preserve existing DE/EN/FR/ES/UK/RU content and contextual language choices. Main UI is DE/EN; unsupported translations are not invented. 11 source video references, seven local posters, explicit user-initiated playback.

## Reusable structure
`v3.js`: shell, navigation, cards/sections, assistant, housing and contextual render.
`v3-content.js`: comparison, knowledge, course/legal content, exams, university and shared application state.
`v3-migration.js`: editorial source editions, timeline, recognition/mentoring, booking, media and source-specific additions.
`tokens.css` and `v3.css`: brand/type/layout contract. Existing app.js/v2.js retain internal demo components and representative specialist workflows. Data is separated in migration-data.js, content-data.js, edition-data.js and media-data.js.

## Source coverage and remaining limits

Locale variants, confirmation states and template pages are not new product areas. The register is outside the public journey.

Not a production system: no backend, authentication, live AI, payment, real upload, reservation or submission. Internal workflows remain representative local demonstrations, not full implementation of every workflow profile. No real university dataset was found in the sources: the three examples are intentionally fictional. Provider entries do not establish current commercial partnerships, APIs, tariffs or commissions.
